Best Practices by Crop

Wheat Cultivation

  • Optimal sowing time: November to December
  • Seed rate: 100-120 kg per hectare
  • Irrigation: 4-5 times during growth cycle
  • Fertilizer: NPK ratio 120:60:40 kg/ha
  • Harvest: When moisture content is 12-14%

Rice Production

  • Nursery preparation: 15-20 days before transplanting
  • Transplanting: 20-25 days old seedlings
  • Water management: Maintain 5cm water level
  • Weed control: Pre-emergence herbicides
  • Harvest: At 80-85% grain maturity
